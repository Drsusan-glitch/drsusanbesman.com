Your first session

What to expect when we first meet.

Your first session is a conversation. We’ll talk about what brought you here, what’s been hardest lately, and what you’d like to be different. I’ll ask about your history and your goals, and you can ask me anything about how I work.

By the end, you’ll have a sense of whether we’re a good fit and what working together would look like. There’s no pressure to commit to anything further.

You don’t need to prepare. If it helps, jot down a few notes about what you’d like to change.

Is this right for you?

Who I work with.

A good fit if you…

  • Are navigating divorce, separation, or the end of a long relationship
  • Feel lost or unsure of who you are in midlife
  • Are facing an empty nest, a career change, a loss, or another major transition
  • Want to rebuild confidence and set clearer boundaries
  • Are co-parenting and want less conflict
  • Live in Florida

A different kind of care may fit better if you…

  • Are in crisis or need emergency support. Call or text 988, or go to the nearest emergency room.
  • Need a higher level of care, such as inpatient or intensive outpatient treatment
  • Are looking for medical treatment, medication, or hormone therapy
  • Live outside Florida and want therapy, since my license covers clients in Florida

If I’m not the right fit, I’ll do my best to point you toward someone who is.
