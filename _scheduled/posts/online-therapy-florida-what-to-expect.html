Maybe you’ve thought about therapy for a while, but the logistics felt like too much: the drive, the waiting room, fitting it into a busy day. Online therapy removes many of those barriers. If you live anywhere in Florida, you can meet with me by secure video from home, your office, or even a parked car.

If you’ve never tried it, here’s what to expect.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person?

For many concerns, including anxiety, depression, grief, relationship difficulties and life transitions, research has found that therapy by video can be as helpful as meeting in person. What matters most is the relationship between you and your therapist, and that can grow just as well on screen.

Who it works well for

  • Women with busy schedules or long commutes
  • Anyone who lives outside Boca Raton but within Florida
  • People who feel more comfortable opening up from home
  • Parents who can’t easily arrange childcare
  • Anyone navigating a move or travel within the state

What you’ll need

  • A phone, tablet or computer with a camera
  • A reliable internet connection
  • A private, quiet space where you won’t be overheard. Headphones help.

Sessions take place on a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform, and you must be physically located in Florida at the time of each session.

How to get started

  1. Book a free consultation. It’s a 15–20 minute call to talk about what’s going on and whether we’re a good fit.
  2. Complete intake forms. If we decide to work together, you’ll fill these out securely through Alma before your first session.
  3. Join your first session. You’ll receive a link. Just click it a few minutes early from your private space.
Support that fits your life is support you can actually keep.

Tips for a good online session

  • Find a spot where you won’t be interrupted, and let your household know.
  • Silence notifications on your device.
  • Have water and tissues nearby.
  • Give yourself a few minutes afterward before jumping back into your day.

Cost and insurance

Private pay is $200 per individual session and $250 per couples or co-parenting session. In-network insurance is billed through Alma, and your copay depends on your plan. The initial consultation is free.
