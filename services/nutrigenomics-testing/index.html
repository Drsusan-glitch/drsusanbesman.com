Nutrigenomics Testing in Boca Raton

How your genes shape the way your body responds to food — so your plan is built on your biology, not guesswork.

Please note: this is a coaching and wellness service, not psychotherapy or medical care. It is offered and billed separately from therapy. If you’re also in therapy with me, we’ll be clear about which service each session is.

What is nutrigenomics?

Nutrigenomics looks at how your genes influence the way your body processes nutrients, responds to different foods, and handles stress and mood. Results can help explain why a general diet plan hasn’t worked for you and point to changes more likely to suit your body.

Available panels

  • Diet & wellness
  • Neurotransmitter / mood
  • Essential vitamins

How it works

Testing uses a simple, non-invasive sample. Once your results are ready, we review them together and turn them into a practical plan for nutrition, movement, and lifestyle. Testing pairs well with health and wellness coaching.

Frequently asked questions

Is nutrigenomics testing a medical diagnosis?

No. It’s a wellness tool that informs lifestyle and nutrition choices. It doesn’t diagnose disease or replace care from your physician.

What happens after I get my results?

We review them together in a session and create a personalized plan based on what they show.
